# Data and Rights

Public reproducibility does not require redistribution of every upstream input.

## Included

- frozen research code;
- public data-source metadata and input contracts;
- derived exhibit and table source data;
- researcher-generated figures;
- nonprivate scientific validation summaries;
- aggregate and derived diagnostics.

## Not redistributed

- downloaded raw Yahoo Finance or FRED market-data files;
- locally retrieved runtime objects or near-raw price files;
- the private June 2023 university project report binary;
- proprietary research files;
- third-party article PDFs;
- local RStudio/Python session artifacts;
- credentials or private author notes.

A clean reproduction may generate public-market inputs locally. Those inputs should not be republished unless redistribution rights have been independently confirmed.

See `06_Public_Metadata/` for source-by-source documentation.
